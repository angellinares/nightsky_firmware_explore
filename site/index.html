NightSky Knob Lanes

Unofficial. Strymon does not make, test or support this mod. You flash it at your own risk.

Control side only. The mod changes the controller firmware: the processor that reads the knobs, buttons, LEDs and MIDI, and runs the sequencer. It does not change the DSP firmware or any audio code. The reverb algorithms stay stock.

Patch

The page patches your own copy of the stock controller update. The page does not upload the file. All work happens in your browser.

    Download the patched 1305_0107_fwu.syx

    Install

    The Strymon Update app flashes the patched file. The app reinstalls the update file from its download folder. You put the patched file in that folder for one install. These steps are for the Windows app.

    1. Connect the NightSky with USB. Open the Strymon Update app.
    2. Wait until the app shows NightSky 1.07 and the REINSTALL UPDATE button is active.
    3. Open this folder in File Explorer:
      %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\StrymonUpdate_e621bjepz8q54\LocalState\Strymon\Updater\V2\r_3\1305\
      The folder name can differ on your computer. If so, search LocalState for 1305_0107_fwu.syx.
    4. Copy 1305_0107_fwu.syx to a safe place. This is your stock file. Patch this copy above.
    5. Put the patched file in the folder, in place of the stock file. Keep the name 1305_0107_fwu.syx.
    6. Set the patched file to read-only: right-click, Properties, Read-only. Otherwise the app can replace it.
    7. Click REINSTALL UPDATE. Wait until the app says the update is done.
    8. Disconnect the power from the pedal, then connect it again.
    9. Quit the app. Make sure that the app is not still running in the background.
    10. Clear the read-only setting. Put your stock file back in the folder. The app then works as before.

    Do not touch 1305_0107_fwd.syx. That file is the DSP update. The mod does not use it.

    Manual

    The stock sequencer plays a SIZE/PITCH value on each of its 8 steps. The mod adds a lane for nine more knobs. A lane holds one value per step for one knob. On each step, every lane with records sends its value. The stock SIZE/PITCH sequence works as before.

    Knobs with a lane: REVERB, DRY, DEPTH, INTERVAL, LOW CUT, LENGTH, SPEED, SHIMMER and HIGH CUT.

    Gestures

    You doResult
    Hold one step and turn a knob, with the sequencer runningThe knob value goes into that step of the knob's lane.
    Press and release steps 3 and 6 together, without a knob turnLive recording turns on or off. While it is on, the ON LED is purple.
    Turn a knob while live recording is onThe value goes into the step that plays now.
    Hold steps 3 and 6 and turn a knobThe lane of that knob is cleared. The knob position becomes its base value. This works when the sequencer is stopped too.
    Turn a knob, no step held, live recording offThe knob position becomes the lane's base value.

    A step with no record plays the base value of the lane. So the knob returns to its base position between recorded steps.

    Presets and power-off

    • Save. A preset save (hold FAVORITE until the LED flashes blue, then pick the location) also saves the lanes.
    • Load. A preset load restores the lanes saved with that preset. A preset without lanes clears them.
    • Back to knob mode. The lanes stay as they are.
    • Power-off in knob mode. The lanes come back at the next power-up, the same as the stock sequence.
    • There is no separate save action. The lanes save only with the stock preset save, and with the stock knob-mode state.

    Limits

    • The Strymon app backs up and restores the stock preset data only. A preset backup does not include its lanes.
    • If the app writes a preset, the lanes saved with the old version of that preset do not load. The mod checks each lane record against its preset.
    • A factory reset of the presets erases the lanes of the presets.
    • The mod is for firmware 1.07 only. The page refuses any other file.

    Restore stock

    Do the Install steps with your stock 1305_0107_fwu.syx in the folder. You can also update to a newer Strymon firmware when one is available. Both replace the mod.

    The lane records stay in the flash memory, but stock firmware ignores them.
